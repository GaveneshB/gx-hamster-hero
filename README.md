> **"Banking that feels like a game, coaching that feels like a friend."**

**GX Hamster Hero** (also known as **GX Buddy**) is a next-generation fintech application designed for the Gen-Z and Alpha generations. Built for the GX Bank hackathon, it transforms traditional banking into an interactive, gamified journey, focusing on building long-term financial resilience through behavioral nudges and AI-driven insights.

---

## 🌟 The Problem & Our Mission

**The Problem:** Many young adults struggle with "invisible spending" (BNPL, micro-subscriptions) and lack the discipline to save consistently. Traditional banking apps are often cold, data-heavy, and reactive rather than proactive.

**Our Mission:** To create a proactive financial companion that:
1.  **Gamifies** savings to make it addictive.
2.  **Protects** essential funds before they can be spent.
3.  **Predicts** future debt risks using behavioral data.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🐹 GX Buddy: Your AI Financial Companion
Your friendly AI coach that understands Malaysian youth and helps you make smarter money decisions every day.
-   **Monitors Spend:** Tracks your habits and provides real-time coaching.
-   **Mood Sync:** The mascot's mood evolves based on your **Financial Resilience Score**.

### 2. 🔮 Debt Risk Radar (The Predictive Nudge)
AI predicts your financial status 30 days ahead and warns you before a cash crunch happens.
-   **Buddy in Action:** *"Heads up! You're on track for a RM50 deficit by month-end. Skip 2 luxury coffees this week to stay green!"*

### 3. 🛡️ Pre-Spending Warning (The "Nudge" Feature)
Analyzes the risk before you pay. If it's impulsive or over budget, Buddy gives you a friendly warning.
-   **Buddy in Action:** *"This coffee is outside your daily budget. Want to choose a cheaper option or save it for your goal instead?"*

### 4. 🔐 Buddy Shield Vault (The Pre-Commitment Nudge)
Automatically shields a portion of your income into Goal Fund and Safety Buffer before you can spend it.
-   **Buddy in Action:** *"Your RM3,000 scholarship just landed! I've moved RM600 to your vault — RM450 for your laptop goal & RM150 for your safety buffer."*

### 5. 💰 Smart Auto-Save (The Frictionless Nudge)
Rounds up your transactions and automatically splits spare change to pay bills first, then your goals.
-   **Buddy in Action:** *"Just bought Nasi Lemak for RM7.20! I rounded up to RM8.00 and split RM0.80 — Spotify bill covered, New Shoes goal growing!"*

### 6. 🏆 Squad Pockets (Social Saving)
Save together with friends for shared goals and stay motivated by squad progress.
-   **Buddy in Action:** *"Your squad is 64% towards Langkawi! Keep it up, Aiman! You're almost there!"*

### 7. 🎮 Gamified Rewards & Missions
Earn XP, level up and collect rare badges for building strong financial habits.
-   **Buddy in Action:** *"Great job! You avoided an impulse buy and saved RM5, +100 XP! You're now a Level 12 Budget Builder!"*

## 🎨 Design Aesthetics
The application features a **Premium Dark Mode** aesthetic with:
-   **Vibrant Accents:** Using a "GX Purple" and "Mint Green" palette.
-   **Motion-Driven UX:** Powered by `framer-motion` for smooth, high-fidelity transitions.
-   **Glassmorphism:** Modern frosted-glass UI elements for a state-of-the-art feel.
-   **Custom 3D-Style Emojis:** Enhancing the "Hero" and "Buddy" themes.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

-   **Frontend:** [React 19](https://react.dev/)
-   **Routing:** [TanStack Router](https://tanstack.com/router)
-   **Styling:** [Tailwind CSS 4.0](https://tailwindcss.com/)
-   **Animations:** [Framer Motion](https://www.framer.com/motion/)
-   **Data Viz:** [Recharts](https://recharts.org/)
-   **Mobile:** [Capacitor](https://capacitorjs.com/) (Cross-platform Android/iOS)
-   **Runtime:** [Bun](https://bun.sh/)
